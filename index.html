WHAT WE BUILD

Services

Every basement starts as a shell. We handle framing, moisture control, electrical, and finishing under one roof.

Wp

Waterproofing & moisture control

Interior drain tile, sump pump install, vapor barriers, and crack sealing so your new space stays dry through every season.

Fr

Framing & insulation

Code-compliant framing around existing mechanicals, plus rigid and batt insulation for a warmer, quieter room.

El

Electrical & lighting

Recessed lighting, dedicated circuits, and panel upgrades handled by licensed electricians and signed off by inspectors.

Ba

Bathrooms & wet bars

Below-grade plumbing, ejector pump systems, tiled showers, and bar setups built for basement-specific drainage.

Fn

Flooring & finish carpentry

Moisture-resistant flooring, trim, drywall, and paint — the layer that makes it feel like a real room, not a basement.

HOW IT WORKS

From bare concrete to move-in ready

Four stages, one crew, no subcontractor hand-offs.

01

Site visit & design

We measure, check for moisture issues, and sketch a layout that works around your mechanicals.

02

Permits & scheduling

We pull the permits, lock your start date, and order materials before a single wall comes down.

03

Build

Framing, rough-ins, insulation, and drywall — with inspections at each required stage.

04

Finish & walkthrough

Flooring, trim, paint, and a final walkthrough so you know exactly what was done and where.
